What this add-on does

The multi-round promo-fraud framework (TPNE-XGB) replays “missed” fraud verbatim into the next round. This add-on replaces that step with an intelligent fraud generator that learns from the fraud which escaped detection and evolves new strategies:

  • new devices / VPN IPs — the attacker moves to fresh equipment instead of re-using it
  • amount & timing shifts — behaviour drifts away from what the detector already knows
  • fraud rings & referral chains — new accounts are wired into collaborative structures
  • victim re-attack — previously abused referrers are targeted again

Two generator modes are available so the original behaviour stays comparable: replay (the baseline clone) and intelligent (GAN or probabilistic synthesis). Every round the dashboard shows feature diversity, feature shift, ring ratio, new edges and the evolved strategy names — full diagnostics.

Honesty notes

  • Ground truth is budgeted: only a few new accounts are “manually reviewed” each round; the rest stay unseen (limited labelling).
  • All data is synthetic; this is an independent implementation and copies no source from the original framework.
