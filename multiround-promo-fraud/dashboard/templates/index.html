About this project

      

        TPNE-XGB implements a multi-rounded adversarial scenario for graph-based
        promo fraud detection. Each round an adversary injects new fraudulent nodes
        (greedy selection + replay/perturbation), and the detector is retrained on
        the growing graph. This dashboard lets you configure, launch, watch live, and
        explore results without touching config files.
      

      

        Intelligent Fraud Generator add-on — instead of merely copying the fraud that
        escaped detection, the INTELLIGENT adversary modifier first analyzes
        the missed fraud (features, degrees, familiar targets), learns/evolves a GAN or a
        probabilistic model over it across rounds, then generates brand-new fraud strategies
        (new device/amount/timing features + fraud rings / referral chains). Diversity and edge
        stats (gen_*) are logged to the results CSV for every round.
